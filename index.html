Splash

A cozy game of weathering the years in Cardinal Grove.

You look after a little town nestled between a forest ridge, a winding river, and restless ground. Each year, decide what to protect, then record the season yourself — which hazard struck and which buildings it damaged — gather what the town earns, and rebuild what's broken. Roll dice at the table, draw cards, or just decide; Splash keeps the books.
